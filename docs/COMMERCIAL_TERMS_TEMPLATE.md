# Commercial Terms Template

> This document is an engineering/product template, not legal advice. Final customer-facing terms, privacy notices, refund rules, tax language, and jurisdiction-specific clauses must be reviewed and approved before commercial launch.

## Product

**Brand:** Songjog (সংযোগ)

**Edition:** Owner Edition

## Access

Access is granted to an authenticated account/workspace according to the active entitlement. Trial and paid periods are controlled by the platform's licensing service.

## Trial

Trial duration: configurable by the platform administrator.

At trial expiry, access to paid-only capabilities may be restricted. Business data is not deleted because of expiry.

## Subscription

A subscription extends the workspace entitlement for the purchased period. Renewal, cancellation, grace period, and refund rules are defined by the applicable commercial plan and payment provider.

## Manual activation

Where a license is sold manually, an activation code may be redeemed by an authenticated account. Codes are subject to the plan's validity and redemption rules.

## Data

Users retain responsibility for the accuracy and lawful use of the data they enter. The platform must provide appropriate export/recovery capabilities and must not intentionally destroy accounting records merely because an entitlement expires.

## Privacy

A separate privacy policy must describe collection, processing, storage, sharing, retention, security, account deletion, and rights applicable to the actual deployment.

## Support

Support scope, service hours, response targets, and supported versions should be defined per commercial plan.

## Changes

Pricing, features, trial terms, and supported integrations may change subject to the final published commercial terms.

## Legal review gate

Do not publish this template as the final legal agreement. Before launch, replace placeholders with reviewed terms covering Bangladesh and any additional target markets.
