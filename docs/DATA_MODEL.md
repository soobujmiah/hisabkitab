# Songjog Domain Data Model

The model is business-scoped. A user may eventually belong to multiple businesses, while each business owns its operational records.

## Identity

- User
- Session
- BusinessMembership
- Role
- Permission

## Business

- Business
- BusinessProfile
- BusinessSettings
- BusinessTypeConfig

## Catalog

- SellableItem
- Product
- Service
- ProductVariant
- Category
- Unit
- Price
- CostBasis
- Barcode
- StockPolicy

A SellableItem may be physical stock, a service, a commission-based service, or another business income item. Cost basis is private business data; selling price is customer-facing data.

## Parties

- Customer
- Supplier
- ContactAddress
- PartyNote

## Commerce

- Sale
- SaleLine
- SalePayment
- SaleReturn
- Purchase
- PurchaseLine
- PurchasePayment
- PurchaseReturn
- ServiceTransaction
- ServiceRefund

ServiceTransaction may carry operational fields such as customer, mobile number, service type, transaction amount, reference, and private commission/cost basis where applicable.

## Money

- MoneyAccount
- MoneyTransaction
- Transfer
- Expense
- Income

## Inventory

- StockMovement
- StockAdjustment
- Batch/Lot (optional)
- ExpiryRecord (optional)

## Documents

- Invoice
- Receipt
- Quotation
- Statement
- DeliveryChallan

Public/customer documents must be generated from an explicitly public projection of business data. Private cost basis and internal margin must not leak into those projections.

## Operational metadata

Most mutable business records should include:
- id
- businessId
- createdAt
- updatedAt
- createdBy
- updatedBy
- status/version where appropriate

Financial records additionally need stable transaction identity, actor identity, source/reference metadata, and audit metadata.

## Money invariants

- currency is explicit
- monetary arithmetic is deterministic
- negative/positive semantics are documented per transaction type
- totals are derived from line items and adjustments
- payments cannot silently exceed applicable balances unless the business policy explicitly supports overpayment
- a posted sale retains the cost basis used to calculate its historical gross profit
- profit is derived from authoritative stored transaction values, never generated by AI

## Profit invariant

For a normal product/service sale:

`grossProfit = recognizedSellingValue - recognizedCostBasis`

For commission-based services, the business may record a transaction amount separately from the business's recognized commission/profit according to the configured service model. External provider balances are not assumed to be Songjog records.

## Ledger invariant

A party's balance must be derivable from its opening balance and posted transactions. Cached balances may exist for performance but must be rebuildable.

## Inventory invariant

Stock is derived from stock movements or an equivalent auditable movement model. Direct quantity mutation without an explainable adjustment record should be avoided.

## Returns and refunds

A sale return must preserve a link to the original sale/line and reverse the appropriate revenue, cost, inventory and party/payment effects. A service refund must preserve the original service transaction and its financial adjustment. Historical evidence must remain queryable.

## Deletion policy

Posted financial records should normally be voided/reversed rather than hard-deleted. Master data such as inactive products/services may be archived.
